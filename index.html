About Me

7 years of studying both design and development

High-school - Web design

2018-2021

Starting in high school, where I took web design as my selected trade, I quickly discovered my passion for developing high-quality websites—both front-end and back-end.
Always eager to learn and try new things, I found myself developing data collection methods for my high school robotics team, creating a public-facing marketing site for a client while still in high school,
and winning first place in SkillsUSA Web Design at District 7 in Pennsylvania.

College - Major In UX/UI Design and Research

2021-2025

In 2021, I started my studies at Harrisburg University of Science and Technology. After spending three semesters majoring in game design, I found myself increasingly interested in the intersection of human psychology and the design of interactive products.
After some thought, I decided to switch my major to User Research to better deepen my interests.
During my time at Harrisburg University, I’ve had several opportunities to gain valuable insight into user research and beyond.

Major internship Opportunity - United Stated Army War College

2021-2025

In the spring of 2025, I took a class titled Special Topics: Games as Simulations. While many of my college courses were important, this one had a profound influence on my career goals.

Throughout the class, we studied tabletop games and their applications in military, educational, and specialized simulation contexts.

Although I had always enjoyed playing strategy games—both digital and physical—as a hobby, I had never truly seen their potential as a tool for user research. As the class progressed, I became deeply interested in this field and leveraged my connections within Harrisburg University to secure an internship at the Carlisle Army War College within the Department of Strategic Wargaming, where I developed a symbol generator for use in wargaming. As well revewiing the prdouct with several high ranking memebers at the college. I was aslo expossed to several real world war games To me, this stands as one of my most important academic achievements.
